## Blockers

### QA-R2-001: A cash deal can't be delivered until the dealer configures an F&I product, records lender-style evidence and fills in 15+ dialogs
- **Area:** dashboard
- **URLs:** `/dashboard/platform/deals/<id>` → `/dashboard/platform/finance` → `/dashboard/platform/delivery`
- **Steps:** Take an approved **cash** deal (round-1 deal `cmvlcxyu0001pld0xutfgyaqn`) to Finance and try to mark it financed so it can be delivered.
- **Expected:** A cash deal goes from approved to delivery with a payment or receipt record and an optional F&I menu. A dealer with no F&I products configured can still sell a car on day one.
- **Actual:**
  - "Mark financed" only appears once funding is current.
  - Funding needs "Review prerequisites", which stays **disabled with no explanation** until all of these exist:
    - a decided F&I **menu**;
    - four compliance checks (identity, OFAC, red-flag, privacy);
    - a signed contract package.
  - On a fresh store, "Present menu" fails with "Configure at least one active F&I product first". So a dealer who sells no F&I products can never deliver a car, and the vehicle stays "pending" forever.
  - With a QA product configured, "Present menu" then failed with the generic "Unable to present menu: Unable to complete the request" until "Record quotes" had been done first (QA-R2-009).
  - The funding dialog makes a cash-deal user tick "I reviewed the signed contract, stipulations, **lender approval**, and insurance".
  - The full path took: present, accept, submit, approve, quote, present menu, decline, 4× compliance, 3× contract handoff, prerequisite review (4 references), funding, mark financed, schedule, arrival, 5× checklist, acknowledgment, complete. That is about 25 dialogs for one cash sale.
  - Once it was all done, delivery worked: the deal is delivered, the vehicle is sold and analytics show $38,000. Also, no payment is recorded anywhere: Payment Transactions stays at 0 for a $39,577 sale.
- **Likely files:** `features/keystone/lifecycle/financePrerequisites.ts:40-65` (`readCurrentPrerequisites` always requires a contract, a menu and 4 checks), `features/keystone/mutations/transitionDeal.ts:96-100` (`toStatus === 'financed'` always requires funding), `features/platform/finance/screens/FinanceDeskPageClient.tsx:720-780` (button gating; no reason shown on the disabled "Review prerequisites"), `features/platform/deals/screens/DealDetailPageClient.tsx:555-562` (an approved deal only links to "Open finance"). Suggested fix: when `structureType === 'cash'` with no products, allow a "Record payment / mark funded" step, make the F&I menu optional (declined by default when there are no products), and show the next required step on the finance row.

### QA-R2-002: The finance desk has seven buttons with no order and no "next step"
- **Area:** dashboard
- **URL:** `/dashboard/platform/finance`
- **Steps:** Open an approved deal on the finance desk.
- **Expected:** One highlighted next action (as on the inventory desk), with disabled actions explaining why.
- **Actual:** Record quotes, Present menu, Record check, Contract handoff, Review prerequisites and Update funding all show at once. - **Inventory filters:** make, condition (Certified 1, New 0) and sort. VDPs show price, VIN, condition and features, and offer test drive, quote, question, financing and trade-in actions.
